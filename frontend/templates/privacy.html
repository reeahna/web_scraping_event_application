Privacy policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

{{ site_name }} lists local events in college towns. You can browse it without an account. This page explains what we collect if you do make one, and what happens when you simply visit.

If you just browse

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The site sets two cookies that it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in (only once you sign in) and one that protects forms against cross-site forgery. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which include your IP address.

If you create an account

  • What your sign-in provider shares. You sign in with Google, Microsoft or Facebook; we never see or store a password. The provider sends us your name, email address, whether it has verified that address, and an account ID so we recognise you next time. We don't keep the provider's access tokens.
  • Your email address and display name, to identify your account and to send the alerts you ask for.
  • What you choose to save: saved events, towns you follow, and your alert preferences.
  • Sign-in records: the time, IP address and browser of each session, and a log of account activity (sign-ins, failed attempts, changes), kept to protect accounts and investigate abuse.

We don't sell your information or share it for marketing, and we only use it to run this site.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Unsplash serves the stock photos shown on events that have none of their own.
  • OpenStreetMap serves the map tiles, and unpkg serves the map software, when you open the map view.
  • Event images and links come from the events' original websites, which load when you view them or follow a link.

Each of these sees your IP address when your browser fetches from it, and each has its own privacy policy. The site is hosted on Render, and emails are sent through an email delivery provider.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from My Account. That permanently removes your email address, name, sign-in links, saved events, follows, alert preferences and sessions, and removes your email address from the activity log. The log keeps only a record that an anonymous account existed and was deleted.
